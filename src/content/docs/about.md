Emerald is a programming language for learning to program. It is statically typed, reads
cleanly, and explains its mistakes: every problem it reports says what went wrong, where,
and how to fix it.

Emerald exists to widen the choice of languages in computer science classrooms. Its
design favors a small set of ideas that fit together, so that what a student learns in one
part of the language holds in the rest.
